About Our Project

Our CPS 3440 Analysis of Algorithms project focuses on delivery route optimization. Delivery companies need efficient routes to reduce travel distance, save time, and improve their operations.

Our goal is to compare different algorithms and study how their performance changes as the number of delivery locations increases.

Our Dataset

We are using the New York City Truck Routes dataset from NYC Open Data. It includes information about streets, boroughs, road segments, road lengths, and geographic locations.

We prepared a cleaned version containing 32,799 records and eight selected columns.

View Original NYC Dataset

Algorithms We Plan to Compare

1. Greedy Algorithm

The Nearest Neighbor approach selects the closest unvisited delivery stop until all locations have been visited.

2. Dijkstra's Algorithm

Dijkstra's algorithm finds shortest paths between connected locations in a road network.

3. TSP Heuristic

A Traveling Salesperson Problem heuristic attempts to improve the order of delivery stops and reduce the total route distance.

Experiments and Results

Our planned experiments will compare total route distance, execution time, and memory usage across different numbers of delivery stops.

Actual benchmark tables and graphs will be added after the algorithms are implemented and tested.
